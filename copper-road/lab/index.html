Chronicle

    Markets

    No merchants yet. Until build step D, goods can't move between towns, so Kingscross and Copperford slowly run out of grain, salt runs out everywhere when autumn salting begins, and Greenhollow's tools wear out. The opportunity board shows what that's worth to whoever fixes it.

    Merchant houses

    The region's traders. Each buys, carries and sells on what it has heard, which is never quite what is true. Click a name for what they know and why they chose their trade.

    Lord Aldric

    What the lord does with his treasury: bread for hungry towns, work for idle trades, festivals, and works that last. Every choice with its reasons.

    Money

    Copper marks, struck at Copperford from the region's ore, and verdigris bits, twelve to the mark: slivers of old copper gone green. (A scute, a shed shell plate of the Old Carrier, is worth twenty marks, but only the tortoise makes those.) Money is counted in whole bits, so the books always balance exactly.

    Opportunity board

    The best wagon loads right now: 30 units, 3 crew eating as they go, prices moving as you buy and sell. This is the lab's all-seeing view. Merchants in step D will only know what letters and rumours tell them.

    Determinism check

    Every build step keeps this promise: 100 simulated days run in seconds, and the same seed always makes the same world. This runs a fresh world for 100 days as fast as your browser can. For seed 1 it compares the result with the same run in Node.js.

    Keys: space pause · 1–5 speed. At 1×, one game day lasts ten real minutes. The simulation core is plain JavaScript and runs the same way in Node for the automated tests.
